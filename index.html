The Royals

Where the night becomes an experience

Designed for those who appreciate the finer things in life, The Royals presents an atmosphere of timeless luxury, complemented by premium dining, signature beverages, captivating live performances, and the finest Bollywood and Desi entertainment.

Every detail is thoughtfully curated — from the elegant ambience and elevated service to the energy that fills the room — creating an experience that is both indulgent and unforgettable. The Royals is not simply a place to spend the night.

Straight From The Floor

Where every performance comes alive

At The Royals, entertainment moves beyond the stage and becomes part of the atmosphere. Experience an extraordinary variety of live performances, each bringing its own rhythm, character and energy to the night.

From electrifying pole dancing and vibrant Punjabi Bhangra to soulful live singing, intimate unplugged sessions, mesmerizing Sufi performances, captivating solo and group dances, and breathtaking acrobatics — every performance is carefully curated to keep the energy flowing.

Different rhythms. Different expressions. One unforgettable experience.
